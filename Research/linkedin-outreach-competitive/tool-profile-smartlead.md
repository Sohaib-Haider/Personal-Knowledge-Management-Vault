## Profile: Smartlead

**Site / pricing**: https://www.smartlead.ai/ · https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing

**Positioning (QUOTED, homepage)**: H1 "Unlock the Full Power of AI Outbound";
subhead "Automate prospecting and close more deals with AI agents and a
deliverability infrastructure built to land emails in the inbox." Hero badge:
"Trusted by 100,000+ Businesses".

**Channel mix**: cold-email-first — unlimited mailboxes/accounts, sender
rotation, email warm-up; plus SmartDialer (AI calls) and SmartAgents. No native
LinkedIn automation on the homepage; LinkedIn is bridged via partner tools
(HeyReach lists "native integrations with Instantly, Smartlead, and EmailBison"
— see [[tool-profile-heyreach.md]]). Classification: email-first with LinkedIn
via integrations (analysis).

**Key features (claimed)**: unlimited mailboxes & storage; built-in setup +
warm-up (DNS/SPF/DKIM/DMARC); Unified Master Inbox with AI reply manager;
SmartAgents; SmartProspect verified-leads; SmartDelivery placement tests;
SmartSenders (rent/buy mailboxes); API & webhooks; white-label add-on ($29/mo
per client workspace, from Pro).

**Safety / compliance marketing (QUOTED, homepage)**: deliverability framing,
not anti-ban: "Our warm-up engine simulates real opens, reads and replies to
make your domain look organic and trustworthy" and "Smartlead handles DNS,
warm-ups and sender rotation automatically." Focus is inbox placement, not
account restrictions.

**Starting pricing (as of 2026-08-09, per https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing)**:
Base $39/mo (2,000 contacts, 6,000 sends), Pro $94/mo, Unlimited Smart $174/mo,
Unlimited Prime $379/mo. Annual billing −17%.

**Observed gaps (analysis/inference)**: no LinkedIn-native outreach capability;
compliance positioning is email reputation/deliverability only (spam avoidance,
implicitly CAN-SPAM/anti-spam) — no consent/unsubscribe product features;
volume-based tiers escalate steeply.
