## Profile: Salesflow

**Site / pricing**: https://salesflow.io/ · https://salesflow.io/pricing ·
https://salesflow.io/features. Note: direct HTTP fetch returned 403 during
research (2026-08-09); claims below were captured from the official pages via a
live search index of salesflow.io content, and are cited to the official URLs.

**Positioning (QUOTED, homepage)**: "The safest Social and Email Outreach
platform to generate quality leads and close deals fast. Built for Sales Teams,
Growing Start-ups and Agencies."

**Channel mix**: LinkedIn + email multichannel in one adaptive flow ("Dynamic
Outreach") with branches on real-time conditions ("If connected", "If open
profile", "If email available", "If email opened"). Email connected via
SMTP/IMAP (Gmail/Outlook/Yahoo) as a secondary channel; no built-in email
finding/enrichment (per third-party reviews, analysis).

**Key features (claimed, features/pricing pages)**: Dynamic Outreach conditional
branching; unified inbox (LinkedIn DMs + Sales Navigator + Open InMails +
email); up to 800 Open InMails/mo; 400 invites/mo (Single User); follow-ups up
to 2,000/mo; auto-withdrawal of pending invites (stay under LinkedIn's 1,200
threshold); randomized activity; native bi-directional HubSpot sync; two-way
Zapier; Google Sheets add-on; team blacklist; white-label + outreach/LinkedIn
Messaging API (Agency); free email Smart Warmup add-on.

**Safety / compliance marketing (QUOTED, homepage)**: "Stay Compliant. Stay
Protected. Your account's safety is our priority. With cloud-based security,
dedicated IPs, auto-withdrawals, and randomized actions, you get a tool designed
to stay under the radar — and play by the rules. We don't scrape data."
Companion claim: "a near 0% ban rate" (features/payment-plans pages).

**Starting pricing (as of 2026-08-09, per https://salesflow.io/pricing)**:
Basic from $99/seat (1+); Starter $70/seat (5+); Pro $39.95/seat (20+);
Agency $29.98/seat (50+); Enterprise $24.99/seat (100+, annual commitment,
billed monthly). 6-month −15% / 12-month −30% (per third-party review citing
official terms). 7-day trial. Analysis: official page shows "$" but one
third-party review states amounts are payable in GBP excluding VAT — currency
not independently verified.

**Observed gaps (analysis/inference)**: email is a secondary channel (no native
enrichment, minimal deliverability infra); no built-in lead database; the
"compliant" narrative is anti-ban/play-by-the-rules, not regulatory tooling.
