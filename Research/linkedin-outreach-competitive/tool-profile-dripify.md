## Profile: Dripify

**Site / pricing**: https://dripify.com/ · https://dripify.com/pricing/

**Positioning (QUOTED, homepage)**: H1 "LinkedIn Automation Made Ridiculously
Simple"; subhead "Reach hundreds of quality prospects per day through automated
LinkedIn & Email campaigns that actually work."

**Channel mix**: LinkedIn + email in the same sequence on every paid plan
("Build smart sequences using a variety of LinkedIn & email actions, delays and
conditions"). Bundles email finder, data enrichment (30+ attributes) and email
verifier.

**Key features (claimed)**: drag-and-drop sequences with 10+ actions &
conditions; hyper-personalized outreach with 20+ variables incl. AI icebreaker;
step analytics; A/B testing (Advanced); dedicated inbox; evergreen leads; team
management with duplicate-outreach prevention; Zapier/Make/HubSpot, webhooks
(Pro).

**Safety / compliance marketing (QUOTED, pricing FAQ)**: "Dedicated IP – a
consistent IP address tied to your account, in your location"; "Human-like
behaviour – actions are spaced with randomised timing and run only inside the
working hours you set"; "Adaptive activity control – your sending pace adjusts
to your account's own activity and history." Nav menu: "Advanced safety
algorithms keep your LinkedIn automation secure and compliant." Daily plan
quotas: Basic 20 connection requests / 30 messages; Pro & Advanced 75 / 100+.

**Starting pricing (as of 2026-08-09, per https://dripify.com/pricing/)**:
Basic $59 ($39 annual), Pro $79 ($59), Advanced $99 ($79) per user/month.
One seat = one LinkedIn account. Enterprise custom. 7-day trial, no card.

**Observed gaps (analysis/inference)**: safety = human-like behavior + quotas,
not regulatory compliance; no white-label below Enterprise; no mobile-proxy
offer equivalent to LGM/HeyReach premium tiers.
