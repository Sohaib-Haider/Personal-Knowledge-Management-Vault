## Profile: La Growth Machine (LGM)

**Site / pricing**: https://www.lagrowthmachine.com/ ·
https://www.lagrowthmachine.com/pricing/ · safety:
https://www.lagrowthmachine.com/linkedin-safety/

**Positioning (QUOTED, homepage)**: H1 "Your GTM stack. Our engine built to
convert."; subhead "Keep the tools you love. Plug them into LGM and turn
multichannel outreach into qualified pipeline." Bumper: "Safely automating
LinkedIn since 2017"; "10,000+ GTM teams already run their OUTBOUND on LGM".

**Channel mix**: multichannel across LinkedIn, Email, X/Twitter and Calls
(channels vary by plan). Includes waterfall email enrichment (9 providers + 2
verifiers), intent data ("Signals"), lookalike company opportunities, and ABM
company database (27M+).

**Key features (claimed, pricing page)**: custom sequences with conditions,
branches and A/B tests; AI "Magic Messages" copywriting; AI voice messages; Real
Chat Mode; social warming; unified multichannel inbox with lead qualification
tags; manual tasks; Slack alerts; HubSpot/Pipedrive sync (Ultimate); API &
webhooks, Zapier, Clay, MCP; 24 custom fields; team management. Priced per
"identity" (a profile running campaigns; replies don't consume an identity).

**Safety / compliance marketing (QUOTED, homepage)**: "100% cloud-based. No
browser extension to detect. LGM runs entirely in the cloud, invisible to
LinkedIn's detection systems."; "Dedicated 5G mobile proxies. Every identity
gets its own mobile connection... Making it indistinguishable from a human
browsing on their phone."; "Smart Safety Limits. Action limits built on 8 years
of real usage data." Safety page: "Most LinkedIn tools treat your account as a
variable they're willing to sacrifice... Our number 1 priority is to keep you
safe." Safety page also lists "GDPR compliancy: We don't just meet GDPR legal
requirements—we make data protection a core part of our processes." — the only
vendor of the six with explicit on-page GDPR language (analysis). Note: homepage
says "5G mobile proxies", safety page says "4G Proxies" — internally inconsistent.

**Starting pricing (as of 2026-08-09, per
https://www.lagrowthmachine.com/pricing/)**: Basic €60/mo per identity
(LinkedIn, Email); Pro €120/mo per identity (adds Calls); Ultimate €120/mo per
identity (adds X + CRM sync) — the page displayed the same €120 for Pro and
Ultimate; treat Ultimate as "€120 as displayed" and reconcile with the vendor
(analysis). Agency (6+ identities) and Custom (from €150) via sales. 14-day trial.

**Observed gaps (analysis/inference)**: per-identity pricing scales with number
of profiles; Ultimate price rendering ambiguous; GDPR is a single marketing
section — no productized consent, lawful-basis, or unsubscribe features.
