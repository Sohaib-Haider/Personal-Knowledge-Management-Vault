## Profile: HeyReach

**Site / pricing**: https://www.heyreach.io/ · https://www.heyreach.io/pricing

**Positioning (QUOTED, homepage)**: H1 "10x your LinkedIn outbound. Unlimited
senders, one fixed cost"; targeted at "agencies, sales teams, and GTM experts...
reach 1000+ leads weekly". "Trusted by 6,500+ companies", G2 4.7 rating.

**Channel mix**: LinkedIn-native multi-account automation with automatic sender
rotation. Email is delegated: "Native integrations with Instantly, Smartlead,
and EmailBison" plus email enrichment credits (100/sender on Growth) that "finds
verified emails for you".

**Key features (claimed, pricing + homepage)**: unlimited campaigns while "each
sender stays within their daily limits and safe usage levels"; "If Connected"
logic; Unified Inbox with sentiment analysis and auto-tagged warm leads; HubSpot
sync ("verified by HubSpot"); workspaces & permissions; multi-brand white-label
(Agency/Unlimited, $500/add-on); API + webhooks; MCP server and CLI for AI-agent
outreach; "Done for you" managed service (custom).

**Safety / compliance marketing (QUOTED, pricing page)**: "Launch campaigns
without restrictions while making sure each sender stays within their daily
limits and safe usage levels." FAQ: "The 'Starter' plan provides a dedicated
residential proxy to each of the LinkedIn senders, while the 'Agency' and
'Unlimited' plans require you to bring your own proxies." Footer: "HeyReach is
not associated with, or endorsed by, the LinkedIn Corporation." Maintains an
Anti-Spam Policy (legal page).

**Starting pricing (as of 2026-08-09, per https://www.heyreach.io/pricing)**:
Growth $79/mo per sender (annual $63); Agency $999/mo for 25 senders (annual
$799); Unlimited $2,999/mo (annual $2,399). Early-stage program for <$250k ARR.

**Observed gaps (analysis/inference)**: email executed inside partner tools (no
native email pipeline/deliverability); steep Growth→Agency price cliff
($79→$999); safety narrative is proxy + daily-limit hygiene, no regulatory layer.
