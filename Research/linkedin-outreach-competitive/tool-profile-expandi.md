## Profile: Expandi

**Site / pricing**: https://expandi.io/ · https://expandi.io/pricing/

**Positioning (QUOTED, homepage)**: "The easiest way for sales teams, lead
generation agencies and recruiters to rapidly scale their outreach and drive
more meetings." H1: "Get the 3X meeting multiplier with Expandi's LinkedIn
automation tool." Site title: "#1 LinkedIn Automation Tool".

**Channel mix**: LinkedIn-native plus email follow-ups in one sequence.
Pricing page: "You can send connection requests, messages, and email follow-ups
in just one sequence." Email is a follow-up channel, not a cold-email engine.

**Key features (claimed)**: Smart sequences with if-then logic; 11 campaign
types; campaign prioritization; A/B testing; duplication security; blacklist;
"Global Smart Inbox" (labels, tags, notes); workspaces + role permissions;
white-label (Agency); webhooks; native CRM integrations. Analysis: homepage
copy states "9 actions & 8 conditions" in one place and "19 actions, 11
conditions" in its comparison table — figure claims are internally inconsistent.

**Safety / compliance marketing (QUOTED, pricing page)**: "Profile Auto Warm-up
... Begin by sending a small number of messages gradually to avoid the risk of
being banned" and "Expandi won't allow you to go over limits to keep your
account safe". FAQ: "We prioritize account safety by recommending the optimal
number of weekly connections based on extensive research and thorough big data
analysis." Footer: "Use of Expandi is at your own risk."

**Starting pricing (as of 2026-08-09, per https://expandi.io/pricing/)**:
Business $99/mo ($79/mo annual, 20% off); Agency custom (10+ seats); 7-day free
trial.

**Observed gaps (analysis/inference)**: safety narrative is anti-ban only — no
regulatory/compliance (consent, GDPR) positioning; email channel lacks
enrichment and deliverability tooling; price billed per LinkedIn account.
