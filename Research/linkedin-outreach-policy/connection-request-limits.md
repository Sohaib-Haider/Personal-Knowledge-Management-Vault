## Purpose

Sub-note of the LinkedIn Outreach Policy research workspace covering
connection-request and invitation limits. Official statements first,
community observations second (never conflated).

## Official — invitations & connections (LinkedIn Help)

- Invitation limits exist and apply to ALL members, Basic and Premium. (official: [LinkedIn Help a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555))
- Reaching the limit → temporary sending restriction, "typically" lasting about one week. (official: [LinkedIn Help a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555))
- Withdrawing pending invitations does NOT remove the restriction; you cannot buy or acquire more invitations while restricted. (official: [LinkedIn Help a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555))
- LinkedIn cannot remove or shorten the wait; Support cannot disclose the type or reason. (official: [LinkedIn Help a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555))
- Triggers: many invitations sent within a short time; many invitations ignored, left pending, or marked as spam; excessive sending suspected to use an automation tool (may suspend or restrict). (official: [LinkedIn Help a551012](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012))
- Maximum 30,000 1st-degree connections. (official: [LinkedIn Help a551012](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012))
- No re-invite to the same recipient for up to 3 weeks after withdrawing an invitation. (official: [LinkedIn Help a551012](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012))
- Restrictions/limits on standard invitations are separate from personalized-invitation limits. (official: [LinkedIn Help a551012](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012))
- Most restrictions auto-remove within one week; repeated suspensions may become permanent. (official: [LinkedIn Help a551012](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012))
- Restriction guidance: first restriction → wait a few hours; multiple in a day → wait a few days; too many outstanding invitations → wait up to one month. (official: [LinkedIn Help a551012](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012))

## Official — the numbers LinkedIn does NOT publish

The Help pages (a550555, a551012) state limits exist, describe their
triggers and durations, and confirm a 30,000 connection cap — but publish NO
numeric weekly or daily invitation cap. This absence is consistent across
both pages. (official: LinkedIn Help a550555, a551012 — observed, no numbers given)

## Community-observed (UNVERIFIED — not official facts)

All figures come from outreach-tool vendor blogs; LinkedIn publishes no
equivalent numbers. Treat as heuristics only.

- Weekly baseline: "about 100" across free and paid; 150-200 for high-trust accounts. (community-observed, unverified: [Taplio blog](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit); [We-Connect blog](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide); [LinkedAPI](https://linkedapi.io/guides/linkedin-connection-limit-2026))
- Some Sales Navigator accounts report weekly limits up to ~250. (community-observed, unverified: [We-Connect blog](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide))
- Reputation gradient (not tied to subscription): new accounts — Taplio reports ~50/week for accounts under 3 months; We-Connect and LinkedAPI report ~50-80/week for accounts under 30 days; established ~100/week; trusted up to ~200/week. (community-observed, unverified: [LinkedAPI](https://linkedapi.io/guides/linkedin-connection-limit-2026); [We-Connect](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide); [Taplio](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit))
- Reset is a rolling 7-day window from when each request is sent, not a calendar week. (community-observed, unverified: [LinkedAPI](https://linkedapi.io/guides/linkedin-connection-limit-2026); [PhantomBuster](https://phantombuster.com/blog/linkedin-automation/linkedin-limit-reset-schedule/); [Taplio](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit))
- Safe daily pacing: ~15-25/day free; 25-40/day for trusted/Sales Navigator; no more than ~10/hour. (community-observed, unverified: [Taplio](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit); [We-Connect](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide); [LinkedAPI](https://linkedapi.io/guides/linkedin-connection-limit-2026))
- Pending invitations: keep under ~400-500; restrictions commonly reported around ~700; one vendor cites a ~1,500 ceiling. (community-observed, unverified: [LinkedAPI](https://linkedapi.io/guides/linkedin-connection-limit-2026) ~500/700; [We-Connect](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide) ~500/700; [Taplio](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit) ~1,500)
- Acceptance rate is the main lever: below ~30% signals spam and throttles volume (Taplio); above ~40% supports higher limits (LinkedAPI). (community-observed, unverified)
- Connection-note length: 300 characters (some sources report 200 for free accounts). (community-observed, unverified: [Taplio](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit) 300; [We-Connect](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide) 200 free / 300 paid)
- Personalized notes on free accounts: ~5/month — matches the official a550555 number. (community-observed, unverified: [Taplio](https://taplio.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit); consistent with official [a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555))
