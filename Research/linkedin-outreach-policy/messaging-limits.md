## Purpose

Sub-note of the LinkedIn Outreach Policy research workspace covering
messaging, personalized connection notes, and InMail credits. Official
statements first, community observations second (never conflated).

## Official — InMail message credits (LinkedIn Help a543695)

Authoritative source: [LinkedIn Help a543695](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a543695/inmail-message-credits-and-renewal-process)

Monthly InMail credits per account type:
- Premium Career: 5/month
- Premium Business: 15/month
- Sales Navigator Core: 50/month
- Recruiter Lite: 30/month

Maximum accumulation:
- Premium Career: 15 · Premium Business: 45 · Sales Navigator: 150 · Recruiter Lite: 120

Rules:
- No subscription plan offers unlimited InMail credits.
- You cannot send another InMail to a member until they respond to the previous one.
- A credit is refunded if the message is accepted, declined, or responded to within 90 days.
- The balance becomes zero if the Premium subscription is cancelled; credits are not transferable.

## Official — Sales Navigator InMail (LinkedIn Help a101030)

Authoritative source: [LinkedIn Help a101030](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a101030)

- All SN tiers (Core, Advanced, Advanced Plus): 50 credits/month, granted on the 1st of each month (UTC).
- Accumulate up to 150; unused credits must be used within 90 days.
- Additional InMail credits cannot be purchased.
- "Interested" and "Maybe later" count as accepted; "Not interested" as declined — all refund the credit.
- An automatic reminder goes to the recipient within 3 days of sending.

## Official — Personalized notes on invitations (LinkedIn Help)

- Basic/free members: personalized message allowed on up to 5 connection requests per month. (official: [LinkedIn Help a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555))
- Premium members: unlimited personalized messages with connection requests. (official: [LinkedIn Help a550555](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555))
- Limits on standard invitations are separate from limits on personalized invitations. (official: [LinkedIn Help a551012](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a551012))

## Community-observed (UNVERIFIED — not official facts)

- Direct messages to 1st-degree connections: no published cap; observed safe ranges ~50/day for new accounts, ~100/day established, 100-150/day trusted; ongoing conversations receive more leeway than cold messages. (community-observed, unverified: [We-Connect blog](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide))
- Messages to non-connections: subject to daily throttles with no public counter; friction signals (warnings, failed sends, verification prompts) appear before a hard stop. (community-observed, unverified: [PhantomBuster blog](https://phantombuster.com/blog/linkedin-automation/linkedin-limit-reset-schedule/))
- Character limits: InMail subject ~200; InMail body ~1,900-2,000; standard 1st-degree message 8,000. (community-observed, unverified: [We-Connect blog](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide))
- Refund practice in the field matches the official rule: any response within 90 days refunds an InMail credit. (community-observed, unverified: [We-Connect blog](https://we-connect.io/blog/linkedin-limits-2026-complete-guide); consistent with official [a543695](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a543695/inmail-message-credits-and-renewal-process)/[a101030](https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a101030))
